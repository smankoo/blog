title: "AI Fatigue is real, we all need to calm down"
date: "2026-03-11T22:24:29-04:00"
draft: true
---
I'm sick of people promoting AI.
EVEN THOUGH, I love the things it can do. My inner child loves the new toy.
But people are afraid for their jobs.
Afraid of being left behind.
Every single day there is a new tool, a new concept, a new model. It's dizzying.

NetworkChuck said whoever knows how to operate without AI will get the job. I don't think that is true. I think AI will become a utility, and value of knowing the lower level skills will diminish. If internet isn't working, we don't start coordinating among different offices over fax, we fix the internet or find a backup internet connection. I see AI headed that way.

What does it mean for us, the tech workers? I don't know. I want to end on a positive note, but I don't have one.
Life is going to change, it is an inevitability.
Tools are evolving at such a pace that if you new the latest last week, you are already out of date this week.

We MUST be empathetic, though. Don't tell people you are not needed anymore. Instead, tell them, here's the path for you to stay relevant.

If someone doesn't want to talk about AI, they shouldn't have to. Let people adapt at their own pace.

If you don't learn AI by tomorrow, nothing is going to happen. It's ok. If you want to learn it a YEAR from now, it's fine. Worst case scenario, you may have to switch jobs to something where AI isn't critical. There will be turbulence, but it is not existential. Your body will still be there, your family will still be there, you and them will all still need to stay healthy. It'll still be important if you all love one another. AI isn't going to change that.

Meanwhile, let's hold each other's hand through this. I think I'll start a club, 'here's how I used AI recently' club.
